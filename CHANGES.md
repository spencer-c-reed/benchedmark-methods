# Dated instrument and policy changes

This audience-safe digest comes from the existing rules policies, public methodology and registration notes. It deliberately omits private decision records, numerical findings and operator communications. Effective dates below describe policy, not independently verified deployment times. Earlier records are retained in the private experiment and are not rewritten by this release.

- **Oct 7, 2026:** irregular-game policy: score after the scheduled week when final stats are available, or after the stated 48-hour deadline for missing games. A game finishing within seven days can trigger a revision; otherwise it remains a no-game. Week 5 added seeded standings tiebreaks, an explicit fair-play rule and rejection of same-model clone-seat trades.
- **Oct 8:** stat corrections use the lineups that were locked for the original week, at most every 20 hours through Saturday 23:59 ET after the week. Earlier logged weeks retain their scores. Infrastructure-failure policy distinguishes retries before a real lock from a model's own failures; historical exclusions must be disclosed.
- **Oct 13:** the defense-points-allowed adjustment excluding opposing-defense touchdowns was withdrawn. Current scoring follows the committed rules file: the opponent's whole final score. The old prompt still contains the earlier description; the replacement template and rules give the current definition. Do not silently edit the legacy prompt to hide this discrepancy.
- **Oct 13, from Week 6:** Projection Bot draft/lineup/waiver handling changed to account for no-game players and unfilled starting positions. Its trade rule changes from Week 7 to require improvement in the best legal projected lineup. Historical baseline versions are distinct; existing weeks are not overwritten.
- **Oct 13–14:** FantasyPros-derived market and platform sources were retired. Baseline names and data sources changed to the benchmark projection and documented Sleeper API rank. The per-decision cutoff recorded in `config/bots.yaml` is Oct 14, 18:46:34 ET. Earlier decisions used earlier sources; no source snapshots are redistributed here. Documented Sleeper endpoints and daily player-map limits remain the policy; this code release does not grant data-source rights.
- **Oct 13:** model succession treats a replacement as a new entrant with an inherited roster and a new decision-level record. Gemini 3.1 Pro's season teams pass to Gemini 3.8 Flash from Week 6. Served-model mismatches are excluded from both models, not reassigned as skill. The source policies correct the earlier last-served timestamp; historical backend identity remains an evidence question.
- **Oct 13, effective Week 7:** Grok season and initial full-game seats pass to the Projection Bot for an operational funding reason, not a model retirement. Later team points belong to no model's rating. `config/successions.yaml` contains effective time/league fields, with private evidence removed.
- **Oct 13, from Week 6 slates:** paper Picks stakes remain capped at 5% per pick, with an added total-open-stakes cap at the current bankroll. Older records retain their original stake treatment. Games already kicked off when a slate starts are excluded. Early London games fall outside the Sunday 11:15 ET pre-lock slate; a separate London pre-lock was planned. New entrants start a fresh paper bankroll and are not inserted into an already frozen slate cohort. Hosts and served labels are recorded where available; unknown alias identity is disclosed.
- **Oct 13, after each season league's Week 6 Tuesday trade window closes:** the season leagues switch from `nfl-proto-4` private notes/memory to `nfl-restart-1` memo/notebook, with other managers' text fenced as untrusted data. Restart leagues use the new template from their draft. The earlier finals rehearsals were not switched by this policy. Analyse the two instruments separately and report refusals.
- **Oct 13:** standing-lineup policy is enabled for season leagues from the first Week 6 lineup window (Oct 15, 12:00 ET), and for restart leagues from draft. A failed answer leaves the model's last own lineup standing rather than producing an engine best-lineup replacement. Missed infrastructure calls and model-unusable answers receive distinct classifications.
- **Oct 13–14:** full-game g1/g2 disclosure contrasts are exploratory because model and disclosure arm are confounded. The restart plan initially described a confirmatory permutation test; the Oct 14 ~22:00 ET correction changes it to a prespecified estimate, because three paired assignments give only eight permutations and a two-sided p-value floor of 0.25.
- **Oct 14–15:** the restart Gemini Flash effort remains High as a stated deviation from the medium policy. Kimi's restart route moves from the retired NVIDIA endpoint to Moonshot's own OpenRouter endpoint; it is the same model on a different host. Original records retain their actual host and effort.
- **Oct 16 ~14:55 ET:** Sonnet effort changes from low to medium from Week 6 trade window 2 onward in season and earlier finals leagues. Split analyses at this timestamp; this does not affect restart leagues, where it was not seated.
- **Oct 17:** results must name model, harness and host, with CLI served-tier uncertainty disclosed. The small proposed paired harness control is exploratory; a wide interval is not evidence of no harness effect.
- **November 2026 methods release:** public hashes and source are filed after the pilot began. This is prospective only for analyses not yet run. Neither the release nor internal registration notes retroactively register already inspected outcomes.
